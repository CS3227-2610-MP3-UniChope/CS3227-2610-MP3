UniChope

Find a consultation, meet with a tutor, and keep the follow-up in one place.

Consultations made easier

UniChope is a role-based consultation booking app built for students, tutors, and administrators. Each person sees the tools for their role, while the backend enforces access and booking rules.

Students

Browse available consultation slots, book a time, and manage your bookings.

Tutors

Manage availability, review bookings, complete sessions, and keep private consultation notes.

Administrators

Manage accounts, modules, Tutor assignments, and review booking activity.

AI stays advisory

Optional AI assistance can draft consultation notes or explain aggregate activity. People review generated text; it does not book, complete, or save anything by itself. Provider access is server-side and subject to application limits. Live provider behavior and deployment availability depend on the team's configured environment.
